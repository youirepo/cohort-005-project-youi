# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and watch their lessons.

## Language

### Purchases

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to have the money for a course purchase given back. It is Requested, then either Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, Cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
Money for a course purchase actually given back to the learner. Only an Approved Refund Request produces a Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback
